# 09 -- Major Battles, Wars, and Conflicts

## Wars (Large-Scale Conflicts)

### 1. Demonic Dao Invasion of State of Yue (魔道入侵越国)
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| When | Han Li age ~32 |
| Belligerents | Demonic Dao Six Sects (魔道六宗) vs. State of Yue Seven Great Sects |
| Scale | Multiple Nascent Souls; entire State of Yue devastated |
| Outcome | Seven Great Sects defeated and scattered; Yellow Maple Valley fled; Han Li abandoned as sacrificial pawn |
| Han Li's Role | Foundation Est. mid -- fought at frontline; eventually escaped via ancient teleportation formation |
| Casualties | Massive; Li Huayuan (Han Li's master) died covering disciples' retreat |

### 2. Scattered Star Seas Civil War (乱星海内战)
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| When | ~Age 126-375 |
| Belligerents | Star Palace (星宫) vs. Anti-Star Alliance (逆星盟) |
| Scale | Multiple Nascent Soul late cultivators; decade-spanning |
| Outcome | Anti-Star Alliance eventually defeated; Six Paths (六道) killed by Han Li |
| Han Li's Role | Core Formation -> Nascent Soul late; ultimately killed Six Paths for Star Palace; secured Ling Yuling's succession |

### 3. Mulan War (慕兰之战)
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| When | ~Age 221-231 |
| Belligerents | Tiannan Cultivation World (正道盟+魔道+九国盟+天道盟) vs. Mulan Spell Warriors (慕兰法士) |
| Scale | Dozens of Nascent Souls; full-scale continental war |
| Outcome | Tiannan victory (narrow); ceasefire after Han Li's intervention |
| Han Li's Role | Nascent Soul early; life-death duels; trapped in Blood Gauze Shroud, broke free; destroyed Bronze Corpse Army; captured Mulan Ancient Lamp; turned tide of war |
| Significance | Established Han Li as Tiannan hero; set stage for his later横跨天南 activities |

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|

| Belligerents | Three 10th-grade demon cultivators + 10,000 demons vs. Minor Extreme Palace (小极宫) |

| Outcome | Minor Extreme Palace breached but not destroyed; demons repelled |
| Han Li's Role | Nascent Soul mid; caught in crossfire; sealed in Void Heaven Hall with Ice Phoenix for ~80 years; breakthrough to Nascent Soul late |

### 5. Demon Realm Invasion / Demon Calamity (魔界入侵/魔劫)
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| When | ~Age 2,332-2,339 |
| Belligerents | Spirit Realm (humans + demon beasts) vs. Demon Realm armies (led by Blood Radiance Sacred Ancestor) |
| Scale | Multi-Mahayana; entire Wind Origin Continent affected; Heavenly Abyss City and Leaning Heaven City as frontlines |
| Key Battles | Leaning Heaven City siege; Heavenly Abyss City final battle |
| Outcome | Spirit Realm victory; First Demon Venerable and Blood Radiance incarnation destroyed |
| Han Li's Role | Body Integration mid -> late; killed three Demon Venerables at Leaning Heaven City; destroyed First Demon Venerable and Blood Radiance incarnation at Heavenly Abyss City; key to victory |

### 6. Mother of Borers War (螟虫之母之战)
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| When | ~Age 2,477 |
| Belligerents | Multi-race Mahayana coalition vs. Mother of Borers (螟虫之母) |
| Scale | Apocalyptic; Mother of Borers at beyond-Mahayana level |
| Outcome | Mother of Borers destroyed with fallen True Immortal He Kang's assistance |
| Han Li's Role | Mahayana; core combatant alongside Baohua; received critical knowledge from He Kang |

### 7. Blood Sky Cataclysm / True Immortal Ma Liang (血天之变/真仙马良)
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| When | ~Age 2,488 |
| Belligerents | Ma Liang (True Immortal from Immortal Realm) vs. entire Spirit Realm |
| Scale | Beyond Spirit Realm limits; True Immortal vs. dozens of Mahayana |
| Three Sieges | 1st: 12 Mahayana + Heaven-Earth Duality Grand Formation + Profound Heaven Imperial Wind Diagram -- crushed; 2nd: 6 Hornet Tribe Mahayana -- 4 dead, 2 wounded; 3rd (final): Han Li + 10+ top Mahayana (including Xuan Jiuling, who killed a 9-headed True Spirit) + Nine Calamity Extinction Art + Two Polaris Dust Grand Formation + 4 Suanni True Spirits + Paradise Brahmic River Realm + multiple Profound Heaven Treasures |
| Outcome | **Ma Liang killed**; Han Li obtained True Soul Pill, Huo Xuzi, and Demon Light |
| Casualties | Enormous -- most allied Mahayana killed; virtually everyone except Han Li died |
| Han Li's Role | Designed as formation sacrifice eye; warned by Crab Daoist; saved by Gold Devouring Beetle King; central to final victory; earned title **"Spirit Realm's #1 Mahayana"** |

### 8. Heavenly Court Final War (天庭终极之战)
| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| When | ~Age 27,000+ (Immortal World sequel) |
| Belligerents | Han Li (Time Law) + Samsara Hall Master (Samsara Law) + Demon Lord (Space-inclined) vs. Gu Huojin (Space Dao Ancestor, Samsara Law controller, Heavenly Court ruler) |
| Scale | Dao Ancestor level; universe-scale implications |
| Outcome | Gu Huojin defeated and killed; three supreme laws realigned; Han Li achieves Time Dao Ancestor |
| Han Li's Role | Protagonist; achieved full Time Law comprehension in critical moment; ascended to Time Dao Ancestor; decisive factor in victory |
| Aftermath | Han Li seals Dao Ancestor origin to avoid Heavenly Dao assimilation; drops to Great Luo peak; retires to Black Earth Immortal Domain |
